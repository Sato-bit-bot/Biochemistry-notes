
***==The important genus of bacterial fish pathogens==***  重要的细菌性鱼类病原体
1.Bacterial fish pathogens belong to different ==taxonomicgroups==**(分类群)**, including various ==genera== and species. The majorbacterial genera associated with fish diseases include Vibrio,Aeromonas, Francisella, Edwardsiella, Streptococcus,Pseudomonas, Flavobacterium, Tenacibaculum,Lactococcus, Acinetobacter, Nocardia, Mycobacteriaand Shewanella among others.
**鱼类细菌病原体属于不同的分类群，包括各种属和种。与鱼类疾病相关的主要细菌属包括：霍乱弧菌、肠杆菌、弗朗西斯菌、爱德华氏菌、链球菌、假单胞菌、黄杆菌、黏着杆菌, 乳球菌、不动杆菌、诺卡氏菌、分枝杆菌和嗜盐弧菌等.**

2.Each genuscomprises multiple species that exhibit varying degrees of ==pathogenicity ==and ==host specificity.==
每个菌属包含多个物种，表现出不同程度的致病性和宿主特异性。

3.==Streptococcosis==, a systemic infection caused by dif-ferent species of Streptococcus including S. agalactiae,S. dysgalactiae, S. iniae, S. parauberis, S. suis [7], andS. phocae has been reported in various fishes worldwide.These bacteria, which are Gram-positive and facultativelyanaerobic, exhibit different levels of hemolysis and arenon-motile with no spore formation.   
Several species of the genus Vibrio such as V. harveyi, V. anguillarum, V.vulnificus, V. alginolyticus, V. parahaemolyticus, V. euro-paeus, V. campbellii, V. owensii, V. ordalii, V. tubiashiiand V. splendidus have been associated with Vibriosis inaquaculture.
**由不同种类链球菌（包括无乳链球菌 [S. agalactiae] 、猪链球菌 [S. suis] 、海豚链球菌 [S. iniae] 、副溶血链球菌 [S. parauberis] 、恶性水肿链球菌 [S. dysgalactiae] 和鳍链球菌 [S. phocae]）引起的败血症性感染——即链球菌病，已在全球多种鱼类中报告。     这些革兰氏阳性兼性厌氧菌表现出不同程度的溶血，并且无鞭毛，无孢子形成。  

**几种弧菌属的物种，如哈维氏弧菌[[[V. harvey]]]、鱼腥弧菌[[[V. anguillarum]]]等。脆弱弧菌、[[[V. alginolyticus]]]、[[[V. parahaemolyticus]]]、[[[V. europaeus]]]、[[[V. campbellii]]]、[[[V. owensii]]]、[[[V. ordalii]]]、[[[V. tubiashii]]] 和 [[[V. splendidus]]] 已经与水产养殖中的 弧菌 病相关联。**

4.A study, reported a ==non-O1, non-O139 sero-type (EMM1) of V. cholerae== exhibiting high mortality inthe freshwater species Labeo rohita, suggesting that strainsof V. cholerae, which do not fit the conventional classi-fication of human pathogens, should also be acknowledged as significant bacterial fish pathogens[3].
一项研究报道了某种非O1、非O139血清型（EMM1）的霍乱弧菌在淡水鱼类Labeo rohita中表现出高致死率，这表明那些不符合人类病原体传统分类标准的霍乱弧菌菌株也应被视为重要的鱼类细菌性病原体[3].

	名词解释: 
	
	 非O1血清型霍乱弧菌: 本群弧菌鞭毛抗原与Ο1型相同，而菌体（Ο）抗原则不同。本群根据Ο抗原的不同，可分为137个血清型（即Ο2-O138），其中一些弧菌能产生类霍乱肠毒素的毒素，而另一些仅产生类似大肠杆菌的热肠毒素，因此少数血清也可引起胃肠炎。
	
	 Labeo rohita是鲤科野鲮属的一种淡水鱼类的拉丁学名，中文通用译名为露斯塔野鲮，也常被称为南亚野鲮、印度鲮，是南亚地区常见的淡水养殖鱼种，在当前上下文中指的是受霍乱弧菌感染、出现高死亡率的淡水养殖物种。


5.Another study reported, ==the draft genome sequence== of V. chagasii strain 18LP [16] and V. jasicida 20LP [17] that has been recently isolated from gilthead seabream larvae (Sparusaurata), highlighting various characteristics associated with opportunistic behavior, such as invasion of host cells, genes responsible for toxin biosynthesis and tolerance, and
resistance to heavy metals[18].

另一项研究报道了近期从金头鲷（Sparus aurata）幼体中分离出的 V. chagasii 菌株 18LP [16] 与 V. jasicida 20LP [17] 的基因组草图序列，揭示了与机会性行为相关的多种生物学特征，例如对宿主细胞的侵袭、负责毒素生物合成与耐受性的基因以及对重金属的抗性 [18]。

6.Vibrio bacteria exhibits ==rod-shaped==, Gram-negative morphology with polar flagella that can thrive in marine and estuarine environments. They prefer warmer water temperatures, which enable their survival, reproduction, and rapid transmission among fish.
Consequently, vibriosis poses a significant threat to tropical aquaculture, especially in the summer [3].

弧菌属细菌呈杆状、革兰氏阴性形态，具有极生鞭毛，可在海洋及河口环境中繁盛生长。它们偏好较高水温，这有利于其生存、繁殖以及在鱼类群体中的快速传播；因此，弧菌病对热带水产养殖构成重大威胁，尤其是在夏季[3]。

7. Important Aeromonas spp. including A. salmonicida, A.hydrophila, A. dhakensis, A. schubertii, A. jandaei, A. veronii, and A. sobria are classified as infectious and opportunistic organisms; they are widespread in freshwater habitats and a wide array of aquaculture settings.

	重要气单胞菌种，包括杀鲑气单胞菌（*A. salmonicida*）、嗜水气单胞菌（*A. hydrophila*）、达卡气单胞菌（*A. dhakensis*）、舒伯特气单胞菌（*A. schubertii*）、简达气单胞菌（*A. jandaei*）、维氏气单胞菌（*A. veronii*）和温和气单胞菌（*A. sobria*），被归类为感染性和机会性病原体；它们广泛存在于淡水栖息地及各类水产养殖环境中。


8. **Aeromonas can cause both chronic and acute diseases in aquatic animals, with a death rate of around 30 percent. Acute disorders include ==hemorrhagic septicemia==, ==skin ulcers==, and ==enteritis [4].**

气单胞菌可在水生动物中引起慢性及急性疾病，死亡率约为30%。急性病症包括出血性败血症、皮肤溃疡和肠炎[4]。

9. Francisellosis infection in fish is caused by Francisellaspp., including F. noatunensis, F. orientalis, and F.philomiragia, which are non-motile, Gram-negative, pleomorphic spherical bacteria that adhere to an aerobic,  facultative, and a sedentary lifestyle. Among these, the pathogenic F. philomiragia can infect both fish and humans,  resulting in fatal systemic infections, skin ulcers, and gilllesions [5].

	鱼类弗朗西斯菌病由弗朗西斯菌属（*Francisella* spp.）引起，包括新弗朗西斯菌（*F. noatunensis*）、东方弗朗西斯菌（*F. orientalis*）和蜃源弗朗西斯菌（*F. philomiragia*），它们是非运动性、革兰氏阴性、多形性球状细菌，营需氧、兼性及静止生活方式。其中，致病性的蜃源弗朗西斯菌可同时感染鱼类和人类，导致致死性全身性感染、皮肤溃疡和鳃部病变[5]。
	
 
10. While most Pseudomonas species are harmless, those that are pathogenic to fish include P. anguilliseptica, P. fluorescens, P. aeruginosa, P. alcaligenes, and P. plecoglossicida. They are ==aerobic==, ==motile Gram-negative rods==. The red spot illness known as "Sekiten byo" is caused by the pathogenic P. anguilliseptica and is highly dangerous to Japanese and European eels [8]. Several species within the Edwardsiella genus, including E. tarda, E. ictaluri, E. anguillarum, and E. piscicida are recognised to be harmful to fish. This genus contains rod-shaped, Gram-negative bacteria. It has been found that widespread infections of E. tarda have devastating effects on both fresh and saltwater fish populations. E. ictaluri, another important species, causes intestinal septicemia in a wide variety of fish, including zebrafish and wild ayu in Japan, in addition to the freshwater and channel catfish species [6]

	尽管大多数假单胞菌种是无害的，但对鱼类具有致病性的种包括鳗利斯顿氏菌（*P. anguilliseptica*）、荧光假单胞菌（*P. fluorescens*）、铜绿假单胞菌（*P. aeruginosa*）、碱假单胞菌（*P. alcaligenes*）和香鱼假单胞菌（*P. plecoglossicida*）。它们是需氧、可运动的革兰氏阴性杆菌。由致病性鳗利斯顿氏菌引起的红斑病（即"赤点病"，Sekiten byo）对日本鳗鲡和欧洲鳗鲡具有高度危害性[8]。爱德华氏菌属中的多个种，包括迟缓爱德华氏菌（*E. tarda*）、鲶鱼爱德华氏菌（*E. ictaluri*）、鳗利斯顿氏菌（*E. anguillarum*）和鱼害爱德华氏菌（*E. piscicida*），已被认定对鱼类有害。该属细菌呈杆状、革兰氏阴性。研究发现，迟缓爱德华氏菌的广泛感染对淡水和海水鱼类种群均具有毁灭性影响。鲶鱼爱德华氏菌是另一个重要种，除引起淡水斑点叉尾鮰的肠道败血症外，还可感染斑马鱼、日本野生香鱼等多种鱼类[6]。

11. Members of the genus Flavobacterium such as F. columnare, F. psychrophilum, F. collinsii, F. tructae, F. branchiophilum, F. hydatis, F. succinicans, F. flevense, F. chilense and F. aquatile are Gram-negative aerobic rods, that demonstrates a widespread distribution in different habitats including diseased fish [9]. Of these, F. columnare which causes columnaris disease, is one of the first documented fish diseases known worldwide. Discoloration, gill necrosis, and fin and skin erosion were the most noticeable external symptoms of disease in fish. Recently, the strain of F. columnare affecting tilapia was reclassified as F. oreochromis [19].

	黄杆菌属成员，如柱状黄杆菌（*F. columnare*）、嗜冷黄杆菌（*F. psychrophilum*）、科林斯黄杆菌（*F. collinsii*）、鳟鱼黄杆菌（*F. tructae*）、嗜鳃黄杆菌（*F. branchiophilum*）、水生黄杆菌（*F. hydatis*）、 succinicans 黄杆菌（*F. succinicans*）、弗莱文黄杆菌（*F. flevense*）、智利黄杆菌（*F. chilense*）和水生黄杆菌（*F. aquatile*），是革兰氏阴性需氧杆菌，广泛分布于包括病鱼在内的多种栖息地中[9]。其中，引起柱形病的柱状黄杆菌是全球最早被记录的鱼类疾病之一。体色褪变、鳃坏死以及鳍和皮肤糜烂是鱼类疾病最显著的外部症状。近期，感染罗非鱼的柱状黄杆菌菌株已被重新分类为罗非鱼黄杆菌（*F. oreochromis*）[19]。

12. Fish ==tenacibaculosis== is caused by several different members of the Tenacibaculum genus, including T. maritimum, T. piscium, T. dicentrarchi, T. gallaicum, T. discolor, T. finnmarkense, T. soleae and very recently isolated T. ovolyticum [20]. These are Gram-negative, marine, filamentous bacteria that cause fish diseases characterized by symptoms such as necrosis, erosion of the mouth, superficial skin lesions, and a lack of coloration in internal organs. Among these T. maritimum has been associated with mortality of numerous marine and anadromous fish species, both in their natural habitats and in captivity [10]. Fish Lactococcosis caused by Grampositive Lactococcus garvieae [21] and the more recently identified L. petauri [22] is an emerging bacterial infection characterized by hemorrhagic septicemia. Isolation of L. piscium from diseased rainbow, trout was the first and sole report of it as a fish pathogen [23].

	鱼类黏着杆菌病由黏着杆菌属（*Tenacibaculum*）的多个不同种引起，包括海生黏着杆菌（*T. maritimum*）、鱼黏着杆菌（*T. piscium*）、海鲈黏着杆菌（*T. dicentrarchi*）、加利西亚黏着杆菌（*T. gallaicum*）、变色黏着杆菌（*T. discolor*）、芬马克黏着杆菌（*T. finnmarkense*）、鳎黏着杆菌（*T. soleae*）以及新近分离的卵黄黏着杆菌（*T. ovolyticum*）[20]。它们是革兰氏阴性、海洋性丝状细菌，引起的鱼类疾病以坏死、口部糜烂、浅表性皮肤病变及内脏器官褪色等症状为特征。其中，海生黏着杆菌与多种海洋性和溯河性鱼类在自然栖息地及人工养殖环境中的死亡率相关[10]。由革兰氏阳性格氏乳球菌（*L. garvieae*）[21]及新近鉴定的 *L. petauri* [22] 引起的鱼类乳球菌病是一种以出血性败血症为特征的新兴细菌性感染。从病虹鳟中分离出鱼乳球菌（*L. piscium*）是其作为鱼类病原体的首次也是唯一报道[23]。

13. These ovoid cocci bacteria are facultatively anaerobic, Gram-positive, non-motile, and non-spore-forming in nature. Rainbow trout (Oncorhynchus mykiss), Nile tilapia (Oreochromis niloticus), amberjack (Seriola dumerili), yellowtail (Seriola quinqueradiata), and cobia (Rachycentron canadum) are some of the notable aquaculture species susceptible to lactococcosis [24].

	这些卵圆形球菌在自然状态下为兼性厌氧、革兰氏阳性、无运动性且不形成孢子。虹鳟（*Oncorhynchus mykiss*）、尼罗罗非鱼（*Oreochromis niloticus*）、高体鰤（*Seriola dumerili*）、五条鰤（*Seriola quinqueradiata*）和军曹鱼（*Rachycentron canadum*）是易感乳球菌病的一些重要水产养殖物种[24]。

14. Important fish pathogenic Bacteria from the genus Acinetobacter such as A. baumannii, A. johnsonii, A. pittii, A. lwoffii, and A. junii are Gram-negative, strictly aerobic, non-fermentative, and non-motile rods. Symptoms such as exophthalmia, dark color of the body, and occasional ulcers on the external part of the body of the infected fish are commonly observed. They are ubiquitously distributed in natural environments and frequently encountered in both marine and freshwater fish populations such as diseased common carp (Cyprinus carpio), channel catfish (Ictalurus punctatus), righteye flounder (Poecilopsetta plinthus), rainbow trout (Oncorhynchus mykiss), zebrafish (Barchydanio rerio), blunt snout bream (Megalobrama amblycephala), south Asian carp (Labeo catla) [12].

	不动杆菌属中的重要鱼类致病菌，如鲍曼不动杆菌（*A. baumannii*）、约翰逊不动杆菌（*A. johnsonii*）、皮特不动杆菌（*A. pittii*）、鲁氏不动杆菌（*A. lwoffii*）和朱氏不动杆菌（*A. junii*），是革兰氏阴性、严格需氧、非发酵、无运动性的杆菌。常见症状包括眼球突出、体色发暗，以及感染鱼体表偶发的溃疡。它们在自然环境中普遍分布，常见于海水和淡水鱼类种群中，如患病的鲤（*Cyprinus carpio*）、斑点叉尾鮰（*Ictalurus punctatus*）、右眼鲆（*Poecilopsetta plinthus*）、虹鳟（*Oncorhynchus mykiss*）、斑马鱼（*Barchydanio rerio*）、团头鲂（*Megalobrama amblycephala*）和南亚鲤（*Labeo catla*）[12]。

15. Three important fish pathogens, of the genus Nocardia namely N. seriolae, N. asteroids, and N. salmonicida are responsible for fish nocardiosis which is a granulomatous, chronic bacterial disease. Bacteria in this genus are nonmotile, Gram-positive, and aerobic. Common clinical indicators of infection in fish include skin ulcers along with several white nodule-like structures on gills, as well as in the kidney, liver, spleen, head, and several other parts of the body. Nocardiosis infection is reported both in marine as well as freshwater fish species which includes northern snakehead (Channa argus) in Korea, yellow tail (Seriola quinqueradiata) in Japan, pompano (Trachinotus ovatus), largemouth bass (Micropterus salmoides), tiger perch (Terapon jarbua) and large yellow croaker (Larimichthys crocea) in China and chinook salmon (Oncorhynchus tshawytscha) in Europe and USA [13].

	诺卡氏菌属的三种重要鱼类病原体，即鰤诺卡氏菌（*N. seriolae*）、星状诺卡氏菌（*N. asteroids*）和杀鲑诺卡氏菌（*N. salmonicida*），是鱼类诺卡氏菌病的致病菌，该病是一种肉芽肿性、慢性的细菌性疾病。该属细菌无运动性、革兰氏阳性且需氧。鱼类感染的常见临床指征包括皮肤溃疡，以及鳃、肾脏、肝脏、脾脏、头部和身体其他多个部位出现的白色结节状结构。诺卡氏菌病在海水和淡水鱼类中均有报道，包括韩国的乌鳢（*Channa argus*）、日本的五条鰤（*Seriola quinqueradiata*），中国的卵形鲳鲹（*Trachinotus ovatus*）、大口黑鲈（*Micropterus salmoides*）、细鳞鯻（*Terapon jarbua*）和大黄鱼（*Larimichthys crocea*），以及欧洲和美国的大鳞大麻哈鱼（*Oncorhynchus tshawytscha*）[13]。

16. Shewanella spp. is recognized as an opportunistic pathogen affecting both humans and fish, exhibiting a broad spectrum of clinical manifestations. Among the noteworthy members of the genus reported in fish include S. putrefaciens, S. xiamenensis S. oncorhynchi, S. baltica, S. morhuae, and S. algae. Shewanella spp. are saprophytic, rod-shaped, Gram-negative bacteria causing gill and skin necrosis, along with ulcerative lesions in the infected fish [15]. The genus Mycobacteria, causing mycobacteriosis in fish and other animals are Gram-positive, aerobic, and non-motile bacilli. Over fifteen mycobacterial species have been obtained from ornamental, cultivated, and wild fish species. Researchers have isolated more than fifteen different mycobacterial species from cultured, ornamental, and wild fish species. Among them, Mycobacterium chelonae, M. marinum, and M. fortuitum are the prominent ones reported in several diseased fish species such as guppies (Poecilia reticulata), yellowtail (Seriola quinqueradiata), koi carp (Cyprinus rubrofuscus), Japanese pufferfish (Takifugu rubripes), gilthead sea bream (Sparus aurata), Atlantic salmon (Salmo salar), red drum (Sciaenops ocellatus) and European seabass (Dicentrarchus labrax). Recently, several novel mycobacterial species such as M. stephanolepidis, M. salmoniphilum, and M. sygnathidarum have been isolated from diseased thread-sail filefish, salmonid and sygnathid fish respectively [14].

	希瓦氏菌属（*Shewanella* spp.）被认为是一种可感染人类和鱼类的机会性病原体，具有广泛的临床表现谱。在鱼类中报道的该属重要成员包括腐败希瓦氏菌（*S. putrefaciens*）、厦门希瓦氏菌（*S. xiamenensis*）、鳟鱼希瓦氏菌（*S. oncorhynchi*）、波罗的海希瓦氏菌（*S. baltica*）、*S. morhuae* 和海藻希瓦氏菌（*S. algae*）。希瓦氏菌属为腐生性、杆状、革兰氏阴性细菌，可导致感染鱼类的鳃和皮肤坏死以及溃疡性病变[15]。分枝杆菌属（*Mycobacteria*）可引起鱼类及其他动物的分枝杆菌病，为革兰氏阳性、需氧、无运动性的杆菌。目前已从观赏鱼、养殖鱼和野生鱼类中分离出超过15种分枝杆菌。研究人员已从养殖、观赏及野生鱼类中分离出超过15种不同的分枝杆菌种。其中，龟分枝杆菌（*M. chelonae*）、海分枝杆菌（*M. marinum*）和偶发分枝杆菌（*M. fortuitum*）是报道较多的主要种类，可见于多种患病鱼类，如孔雀鱼（*Poecilia reticulata*）、五条鰤（*Seriola quinqueradiata*）、锦鲤（*Cyprinus rubrofuscus*）、日本河鲀（*Takifugu rubripes*）、金头鲷（*Sparus aurata*）、大西洋鲑（*Salmo salar*）、眼斑拟石首鱼（*Sciaenops ocellatus*）和欧洲海鲈（*Dicentrarchus labrax*）。近期，从患病单棘鲀、鲑科鱼类及海龙科鱼类中分别分离出若干新种分枝杆菌，如 *M. stephanolepidis*、*M. salmoniphilum* 和 *M. sygnathidarum* [14]。

17. **Other important bacterial fish pathogens**
In recent times, certain bacteria with opportunistic tendencies have been recognized as the primary factors behind significant disease outbreaks in aquaculture settings [25]. Some of the most notable fish pathogens among them include ==Enterococcus faecalis==, ==Piscirickettsia salmonis==, ==Renibacterium salmoninarum==, Photobacterium damselae subsp. Piscicida, Yersinia ruckeri, and Moritella viscosa, which severely impact the aquaculture industry worldwide.

Enterococcus faecalis: First observed in the yellow tail (Seriola quinqueradiata) in Japan, Enterococcus spp. has been isolated from different fishes such as tilapia (Oreochromis niloticus) and turbot (Scophthalmus maximus). Streptococcal infections in tilapia caused by E. faecalis have been documented in Egyptian and Thai lakes as well as from healthy and infected fish from Bangladesh [26].

Piscirickettsia salmonis: Piscirickettsiosis/Salmonid rickettsial disease (SRS) attributed to Piscirickettsia salmonis stands as a serious disease leading to huge economic setbacks for the aquaculture sector. Initially isolated from infected coho salmon, piscirickettsiosis has since then been found in rainbow trout (Oncorhynchus mykiss) and Atlantic salmon (Salmo salar). It has predominantly been documented in marine and brackish ecosystems, with minimal occurrences reported in freshwater environments [27].

Renibacterium salmoninarum: Salmonid fish are notably prone to an ailment referred to as bacterial kidney disease (BKD), attributed to the fish pathogen Renibacterium salmoninarum. Reported mortality rates of approximately 80% and 40% have been documented in pacific salmon (Oncorhynchus spp.) and Atlantic salmon (Salmo salar), respectively [28].

Photobacterium damselae subsp. piscicida: Photobacteriosis disease, caused by the Gram-negative bacterium Photobacterium damselae subsp. piscicida, afflicts a multitude of fish species, inducing a severe septicemic condition observed in various warm water marine fish species globally. It has been isolated from economically important fish species, such as white perch (Morone americanus), striped bass (Morone saxatilis), sea bream (Sparus aurata), sole (Solea senegalensis and Solea solea), cobia (Rachycentron canadum) and yellowtail (Seriola quinqueradiata) primarily produced in China and Japan, respectively and European sea bass (Dicentrarchus labrax) cultured in Mediterranean countries [29].

Yersinia ruckeri: Salmonids (trout and salmon) and other cold-water fish are susceptible to a bacterial infection called enteric redmouth disease (ERM), also known as yersiniosis [30]. Additionally, it has been found in other farmed fish hosts such as channel catfish (Ictalurus punctatus), Atlantic cod (Gadus morhua L.) as well as wild fish species [31].

Moritella viscosa: The Gram-negative bacterium, Moritella viscosa, causes winter ulcer disease, which is characterised by massive, chronic ulcers in the skin of different number of fish species. It is linked to cold marine water conditions (i.e., below 10 °C) and results in significant, persistent ulcers in rainbow trout (Oncorhynchus mykiss), halibut (Hippoglossus hippoglossus), Atlantic salmon (Salmo salar), lumpfish (Cyclopterus lumpus), Atlantic cod (Gadus morhua), turbot (Scophthalmus maximus) [32].

	其他重要的鱼类细菌性病原体

近年来，某些具有机会性致病倾向的细菌已被认定为水产养殖环境中重大疾病暴发的主要因素[25]。其中最引人注目的鱼类病原体包括粪肠球菌（*Enterococcus faecalis*）、鲑鱼立克次体（*Piscirickettsia salmonis*）、肾杆菌（*Renibacterium salmoninarum*）、杀鱼光合杆菌（*Photobacterium damselae* subsp. *piscicida*）、鲁氏耶尔森菌（*Yersinia ruckeri*）和莫氏枝霉菌（*Moritella viscosa*），它们对全球水产养殖业造成了严重影响。

**粪肠球菌**（*Enterococcus faecalis*）：肠球菌最早在日本五条鰤（*Seriola quinqueradiata*）中被发现，后从罗非鱼（*Oreochromis niloticus*）和大菱鲆（*Scophthalmus maximus*）等不同鱼类中分离出来。在埃及和泰国湖泊以及孟加拉国的健康和患病鱼类中，已记录了由粪肠球菌引起的罗非鱼链球菌感染[26]。

**鲑鱼立克次体**（*Piscirickettsia salmonis*）：由鲑鱼立克次体引起的鱼类立克次体病/鲑科鱼类立克次体病（SRS）是一种严重疾病，给水产养殖业造成了巨大的经济损失。该病最初从患病的银大麻麻哈鱼中分离，此后在虹鳟（*Oncorhynchus mykiss*）和大西洋鲑（*Salmo salar*）中均有发现。该病主要在海洋和半咸水生态系统中报道，淡水环境中仅有少量病例报告[27]。

**肾杆菌**（*Renibacterium salmoninarum*）：鲑科鱼类易患一种称为细菌性肾病（BKD）的疾病，由病原体肾杆菌引起。据报道，太平洋鲑（*Oncorhynchus* spp.）的死亡率约为80%，大西洋鲑（*Salmo salar*）的死亡率约为40%[28]。

**杀鱼光合杆菌**（*Photobacterium damselae* subsp. *piscicida*）：由革兰氏阴性细菌杀鱼光合杆菌引起的光合杆菌病，影响多种鱼类，在全球多种暖水性海水鱼类中引发严重的败血症。已从多种经济重要鱼类中分离出该菌，如白鲈（*Morone americanus*）、条纹鲈（*Morone saxatilis*）、海鲷（*Sparus aurata*）、鳎（*Solea senegalensis* 和 *Solea solea*）、军曹鱼（*Rachycentron canadum*）和五条鰤（*Seriola quinqueradiata*，主要在中国和日本养殖）以及地中海国家养殖的欧洲海鲈（*Dicentrarchus labrax*）[29]。

**鲁氏耶尔森菌**（*Yersinia ruckeri*）：鲑科鱼类（鳟鱼和鲑鱼）及其他冷水鱼类易感一种称为传染性鲑鱼腹水病（ERM）的细菌感染，也称耶尔森菌病[30]。此外，该病还在斑点叉尾鮰（*Ictalurus punctatus*）、大西洋鳕（*Gadus morhua* L.）等养殖鱼类及野生鱼类中被发现[31]。

	**莫氏枝霉菌**（*Moritella viscosa*）：革兰氏阴性细菌莫氏枝霉菌引起冬季溃疡病，以多种鱼类皮肤出现大面积慢性溃疡为特征。该病与寒冷海水环境（即低于10°C）相关，可导致虹鳟（*Oncorhynchus mykiss*）、大菱鲆（*Hippoglossus hippoglossus*）、大西洋鲑（*Salmo salar*）、圆鳍鱼（*Cyclopterus lumpus*）、大西洋鳕（*Gadus morhua*）和大菱鲆（*Scophthalmus maximus*）等鱼类出现严重且持续的溃疡[32]。

18. The inadequacies of conventional methods for identifying bacterial fish pathogens

Bacterial infections present substantial challenges to the aquaculture sector, resulting in financial repercussions and potential public health concerns. Accurate and timely identification of fish pathogens is crucial for effective disease management. Traditional techniques for bacterial identification, such as culture-based methods and biochemical tests, have traditionally found extensive application in bacterial fish disease diagnosis in the past. However, these techniques have several limitations that hinder their effectiveness in providing accurate and timely identification. The following are some of the key limitations of traditional techniques:

Slow and Time-Consuming: Traditional bacterial identification methods often involve culturing the pathogen on specific media and performing biochemical tests, which can be time-consuming. It may take several days or even weeks to obtain conclusive results, which delays the diagnosis and subsequent implementation of control measures [33].

Limited Sensitivity: Some fish pathogens are fastidious and require specific growth conditions, that pose challenges when attempting their isolation and identification through conventional techniques. These methods might fail to detect low levels of bacterial pathogens or identify slow-growing or non-culturable strains [34], leading to false-negative results.

Limited Specificity: Traditional techniques rely on phenotypic characteristics and biochemical reactions to identify bacterial pathogens. However, some bacteria may exhibit similar phenotypic features, leading to misidentification or confusion between closely related species. This can result in incorrect diagnoses and inappropriate treatment strategies.

Inability to Detect Mixed Infections: Bacterial fish diseases can involve multiple pathogens, and traditional techniques may fail to detect multiple bacterial species in a single specimen [35]. This limitation hinders the understanding of complex disease dynamics and may lead to incomplete or inaccurate diagnosis.

Lack of Genetic Information: Traditional techniques do not provide comprehensive genetic information about bacterial pathogens. This information is crucial for understanding the virulence factors, genetic diversity, and antimicrobial resistance profiles of bacterial fish pathogens [36]. Without genetic data, it becomes challenging to develop targeted control strategies and monitor the emergence of new strains.

Requirement for Culturable Organisms: Traditional techniques heavily rely on the ability to culture the bacteria in the laboratory [37]. However, some bacterial species, especially unculturable or difficult-to-culture strains, cannot be identified using these methods. This limitation restricts the identification and characterization of certain fish pathogens.

	识别鱼类细菌性病原体的传统方法之不足

细菌性感染对水产养殖业构成了重大挑战，造成了经济损失并引发了潜在的公共卫生问题。准确及时地鉴定鱼类病原体对于有效的疾病管理至关重要。传统的细菌鉴定技术，如基于培养的方法和生化试验，过去在鱼类细菌性疾病诊断中得到了广泛应用。然而，这些技术存在诸多局限性，制约了其提供准确及时鉴定结果的能力。以下是传统技术的一些主要局限性：

**耗时且周期长**：传统的细菌鉴定方法通常需要将病原体在特定培养基上培养并进行生化试验，这一过程往往十分耗时。获取最终结果可能需要数天甚至数周时间，从而延误了诊断及后续防控措施的实施[33]。

**灵敏度有限**：某些鱼类病原体对培养条件要求苛刻，需要特定的生长条件，这给通过常规技术进行分离和鉴定带来了挑战。这些方法可能无法检测到低浓度的细菌病原体，或鉴定出生长缓慢及不可培养的菌株[34]，导致假阴性结果。

**特异性有限**：传统技术依赖表型特征和生化反应来鉴定细菌病原体。然而，某些细菌可能表现出相似的表型特征，导致近缘种之间的误鉴定或混淆。这可能导致错误的诊断和不恰当的治疗策略。

**无法检测混合感染**：鱼类细菌性疾病可能涉及多种病原体，而传统技术可能无法在单一样本中检测到多种细菌种类[35]。这一局限性阻碍了对复杂疾病动态的理解，并可能导致诊断不完整或不准确。

**缺乏遗传信息**：传统技术无法提供关于细菌病原体的全面遗传信息。这些信息对于理解鱼类细菌性病原体的毒力因子、遗传多样性和耐药性特征至关重要[36]。没有遗传数据，制定针对性的防控策略和监测新菌株的出现将面临巨大困难。

**依赖可培养微生物**：传统技术严重依赖在实验室中培养细菌的能力[37]。然而，某些细菌种类，特别是不可培养或难以培养的菌株，无法通过这些方法进行鉴定。这一局限性限制了对某些鱼类病原体的鉴定和表征。

19. These drawbacks have prompted the exploration of genomics as an alternative approach for bacterial fish pathogen study. These methods offer higher sensitivity, specificity, and the ability to detect and characterize pathogens directly from clinical samples, bypassing the need for culture. They also provide valuable genotypic information, enabling comprehensive analysis of bacterial populations and facilitating the development of targeted control measures [38].

	上述不足促使基因组学作为一种替代方法被引入鱼类细菌性病原体的研究中。这些方法具有更高的灵敏度和特异性，能够直接从临床样本中检测和表征病原体，无需经过培养过程。同时，它们还能提供有价值的基因型信息，使得对细菌种群进行全面分析成为可能，并有助于制定针对性的防控措施[38]。

20. Genomic approaches for bacterial fish pathogen identification and classification

High‑throughput sequencing in understanding bacterial fish pathogen genomics

Genomic approaches have revolutionized the process of identifying and categorizing bacterial fish pathogens by providing comprehensive and accurate insights into their genetic makeup. These approaches leverage high-throughput sequencing technologies and advanced bioinformatics tools to analyze the genomic data of pathogens, enabling rapid and precise identification at various taxonomic levels [39]. A very recent study employed the Illumina platform (MiSeq) to ascertain the evolutionary relationships among the Shewanella species obtained from fish specimens. The results of the study indicate that, in contrast to the standard method of 16S rRNA amplicon sequencing, which is considered the standard approach, the use of whole-genome sequence (WGS) analysis allowed for the taxonomic categorization and evaluation of the diversity of the Shewanella spp. strains. This study indicates that the species S. oncorhynchi, which was newly identified, is the most commonly observed Shewanella species in freshwater fishes [40].

	鱼类细菌性病原体鉴定与分类的基因组学方法

高通量测序在理解鱼类细菌性病原体基因组学中的应用

基因组学方法通过全面、准确地揭示鱼类细菌性病原体的遗传构成，彻底改变了其鉴定和分类的过程。这些方法利用高通量测序技术和先进的生物信息学工具分析病原体的基因组数据，实现了在不同分类学水平上的快速精准鉴定[39]。一项最新研究采用Illumina平台（MiSeq）来确定从鱼类样本中获得的希瓦氏菌种之间的进化关系。研究结果表明，与被视为标准方法的16S rRNA扩增子测序相比，全基因组序列（WGS）分析能够实现对希瓦氏菌属菌株的分类学归类和多样性评估。该研究指出，新近鉴定的鳟鱼希瓦氏菌（*S. oncorhynchi*）是淡水鱼类中最常见的希瓦氏菌种[40]。

21. Whole‑genome sequencing (WGS) in bacterial fish pathogen identification

Whole-genome sequencing (WGS) entails sequencing the entire genome of a bacterial pathogen to reveal all its genetic information. Researchers can correctly identify the pathogen down to the species or strain level by comparing the derived genomic sequence with reference databases. The discovery of virulence genes and other genomic factors linked to pathogenicity is another benefit of WGS. The diversity and evolutionary history of bacterial fish pathogens can be better comprehended with the use of this data. Disease emergence and spread can be monitored in both individual fish farms and between farms using WGS, just as it can be done in hospitals [41]. Many different bacterial pathogens found in aquaculture have already had their complete reference genomes sequenced which have been deposited by different regions around the globe since 2004 (Fig. 2A). 	全基因组测序（WGS）在鱼类细菌性病原体鉴定中的应用

全基因组测序（WGS）是对细菌性病原体的整个基因组进行测序，以揭示其全部遗传信息。研究人员通过将获得的基因组序列与参考数据库进行比对，能够准确地将病原体鉴定到种或菌株水平。WGS的另一项优势是能够发现与致病性相关的毒力基因及其他基因组因素。利用这些数据，可以更好地理解鱼类细菌性病原体的多样性和进化历史。WGS可用于监测个体鱼场内部以及鱼场之间的疾病暴发和传播，其应用方式与医院中的监测类似[41]。自2004年以来，水产养殖中发现的多种不同细菌性病原体的完整参考基因组已由全球不同地区完成测序并提交至公共数据库（图2A）。图2B中的数据展示了2004年至2023年7月期间鱼类细菌性病原体全基因组序列提交的时间趋势，反映了多年来基因组学研究和数据资源的演进历程。
